# Ibn Al-Arab - Bilingual Meal Delivery Platform

## Project Summary
Ibn Al-Arab is a bilingual meal delivery platform that allows customers to browse, order, and receive traditional Arabic meals with full English/Arabic language support.

## Purpose
To provide a seamless meal ordering experience for customers who enjoy traditional Arabic cuisine, with full support for both English and Arabic languages throughout the application.

## Target Users
- Arabic food enthusiasts
- Customers seeking authentic traditional meals
- Users who prefer Arabic language interfaces
- International customers who want English support
